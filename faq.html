The Process
Every session is collaborative and tailored to your needs. We may review OT reports and IEP goals together, discuss home strategies, problem-solve a specific challenge you're facing, or build out your personalized toolbox. I teach you the what, how, and why of OT — so you leave every session with something you can use immediately.
Every family is different. Some families benefit enormously from just a few focused sessions. Others prefer ongoing support as their child grows. After our intake, I'll share my honest sense of what would serve your family best — with no pressure to commit to more than you need. When you're ready to taper down, we shift to bi-weekly, then monthly, then quarterly check-ins to maintain growth.
