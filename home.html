What Occupational Therapy Really Is

Occupational therapy is singular among allied health professions — it evaluates, observes, teaches, adapts, and modifies how a child functions across every real-world situation. OT looks at the whole child: how their brain processes sensation, how they plan and execute tasks, how they regulate their emotions, and how all of that connects to daily life. OTs connect the dots across brain function like no other discipline can.
